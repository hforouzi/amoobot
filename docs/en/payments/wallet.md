# Customer Wallet

Every customer can have a wallet: a balance in Toman that is topped up with the shop's payment methods and spent on new services, renewals and extra traffic. The wallet is **off by default**.

## Settings (Settings → Wallet, super admin)

- **Enable the wallet**: shows "Wallet" in the bot menu and on the Mini App "My services" page.
- **Allow paying orders from the wallet** (on by default once the wallet is enabled).
- **Minimum / maximum top-up** and **quick amounts** (comma separated, each within the range).

## How it works

- **Ledger.** Every movement is an append-only row (top-up, purchase, refund, adjustment, reward, reversal) with the balance after it. A mistake is corrected with a new row, never by editing. The cached balance must equal the ledger sum: `php bin/console app:wallet:reconcile` reports any mismatch (exit code 1) and never fixes anything. It also checks payments against the ledger and lists, with their ids: a confirmed top-up without its credit, a top-up credit whose payment is not confirmed, a wallet payment without its debit, a rejected payment whose debit was never given back, a debit given back while its payment is still live, a purchase debit without a payment, a held debit whose amount is not its payment's amount (`debit_amount_mismatch`), a wallet payment still pending (its debit held) while its order no longer waits for payment (`pending_wallet_payment_order_not_waiting`: paid otherwise, cancelled or expired, so nothing would give the debit back), a wallet payment in provisioning failed or in review whose debit is held while its order failed, was cancelled or expired (`held_debit_order_closed`, e.g. after an edit of the order's status), and one whose debit is held while another payment of the same order is confirmed (`held_debit_order_paid_otherwise`: the customer paid twice). It also checks reseller orders and their refunds (see [Resellers](../resellers.md#monitoring)).
- **Top-up.** A top-up is an order of type "wallet top-up" paid like any order (card to card with a receipt, Zibal, custom API, NOWPayments, plugins). The wallet is credited once, when the payment is confirmed. A rejected, failed, expired or cancelled payment never credits; money paid after a cancel or an expiry waits in "Needs review" and is credited only when an admin approves it.
- **What a top-up credits.** What was actually received, never more than the ordered amount. If a gateway reports a little less than expected (within its underpayment tolerance, e.g. 1% for crypto) or an admin approves an underpaid top-up, the wallet gets `floor(ordered × received ÷ expected)` Toman; to give the full amount anyway, add the difference as a manual adjustment. An overpayment credits the ordered amount and alerts the admins. A card-to-card top-up approved from its receipt credits the ordered amount. The ledger row's note shows received vs credited. If crediting fails after the gateway confirmed the money (a database error), nothing is changed, the payment stays pending for the next verification or an admin's approval, and the admins get one Telegram alert for that payment.
- **Paying from the wallet.** Offered only when the balance (plus the credit limit) covers the whole amount; partial wallet payment is not supported. The order gets a payment of method "wallet" and is confirmed like any other payment. If creating the service fails, the usual "Retry" works and never takes the money twice. If an admin rejects the payment (with or without "refunded"), or an unsettled wallet payment is cancelled or expires, the money goes back to the wallet once. When another payment pays the order (e.g. an earlier gateway or card-to-card payment the customer completed anyway), every other wallet payment of the order gives its money back once, whatever its state (pending, in review, provisioning failed); so does every wallet payment of an order that fails because an admin rejected another of its payments. Exception: while a wallet payment of the order waits for a retry (provisioning failed), rejecting another payment of it (e.g. a fake card-to-card receipt) rejects only that payment; the order stays as it is and the wallet payment keeps its money for the retry (rejecting the wallet payment itself fails the order and gives the money back). If a gateway payment's service creation fails while another payment of the order (e.g. an admin's retry of the wallet payment) has just paid it, the gateway payment is not recorded as "provisioning failed": it goes to "Needs review" as a duplicate, for a refund. While a wallet payment of an order still holds its money (pending, in review, or waiting for a retry after a failed provisioning), no other payment is started for that order and no card-to-card receipt is accepted for it (bot and Mini App): the customer is told it is already paid from the wallet. A receipt never moves an order out of "provisioning failed".
- **Confirmation.** Before a purchase is paid from the wallet (new service, renewal, extra traffic) the customer confirms "Pay X Toman from your wallet? Balance Y → Z", computed from the database at that moment. In the bot: **✅ Confirm** pays, **❌ Cancel** shows the order's payment methods again and changes nothing. In the Mini App the server asks for the confirmation (Telegram's dialog); only OK pays. A double tap pays once.
- **A wallet payment that did not finish.** If the wallet was debited but confirming the order did not finish (a timeout or an error while the service was being created), the order keeps that one wallet payment. Until it is settled, another payment method cannot be started for the order: the customer is asked to tap "Pay from wallet" again (it reuses the same debit, nothing is taken twice) or to cancel the order (the amount goes back to the wallet). This is deliberate: starting a gateway payment beside a debited wallet payment could charge the customer twice.
- **No "paid late" for wallet payments.** A wallet payment that was cancelled or expired before it was settled already gave its money back; it is never parked in "Needs review" as a late payment. A wallet payment is confirmed (by itself, by an admin's approval or by a retry) only while its debit is still taken; otherwise the admin sees "Not confirmed: this wallet payment has no wallet debit any more" and should reject it.
- **Credit limit.** How far below zero a balance may go (0 by default). It cannot be set lower than the credit already in use.
- **Frozen wallet.** Cannot be used to pay or be topped up by the customer; payments already made are still credited and admins can still adjust. A wallet payment whose money was already taken before the freeze (its order was not confirmed yet) still completes with that one debit; the money is already out of the wallet, so refusing it would only hold it. Freezing stops every new wallet payment.

## Monitoring

- Run `php bin/console app:wallet:reconcile --env=prod` as the web user once a day (e.g. a cron line `sudo -u www-data php /var/www/amoobot/bin/console app:wallet:reconcile --env=prod`) and alert on exit code 1. It only reads; each mismatch it lists is corrected by a person (an admin adjustment or a payment decision).
- **Stuck payments.** A payment still in "provisioning failed", or in review with its wallet money taken, more than 6 hours after it happened is alerted to the admins again (Telegram, with the payment's buttons), at most once a day per payment, by the scheduled `app:automation:run --only=orders` job (every 10 minutes; not behind an automation switch). `--dry-run` only counts.
- The post-deploy check (`sudo /usr/local/lib/amoobot/post-deploy-check.sh`) runs it too and prints an `INFO wallet-reconcile` line: a `WARNING` when it finds mismatches, which never changes the check's result.

## Upgrading (run the migrations)

- `Version20261024090000`: creates `wallet` and `wallet_transaction` (new, empty tables; instant). **Irreversible once the wallet has been used**: rolling it back is refused while the ledger has any row, or a wallet has a balance, a credit limit or is frozen (dropping the tables would delete every balance, its history or the admin's decision).
- `Version20261024090100`: makes `order.plan_id` nullable. **It rebuilds the `order` table**: online on MySQL 5.6+ / MariaDB 10.0+ (`ALGORITHM=INPLACE, LOCK=NONE`, orders keep working), but run it off-peak with disk space for a copy of `order`. If the server refuses the online mode, the statement fails without changing anything; then run ``ALTER TABLE `order` MODIFY plan_id INT DEFAULT NULL`` in a maintenance window. **Irreversible once a top-up order exists**: rolling it back is refused while any order has no plan.
- `Version20261025090000`: adds `payment.wallet_credit_alerted_at` (nullable, no default; instant on MariaDB 10.3+).
- `Version20261025090100`: the wallet ledger's foreign keys refuse deletes instead of cascading (no data is changed). **Rolling it back is refused**, before any key is touched, while the ledger has any row or a wallet has a balance, a credit limit or is frozen: with cascading keys again, deleting a customer would erase that money or the admin's decision.
- `Version20261026090000`: adds `payment.stuck_reminded_at` (nullable, no default; instant on MariaDB 10.3+), for the stuck-payment reminder.
- **Rolling back: one version at a time** (`php bin/console doctrine:migrations:migrate prev`, then check, then the next). MariaDB/MySQL cannot roll back schema changes, so a multi-step rollback is not atomic: when one step refuses (e.g. `Version20261024090100` while a top-up order exists), the later migrations it already reverted stay reverted. Take a database backup first. On a server whose wallet was used, rolling back stops at `Version20261025090100` (it is refused while `wallet_transaction` has rows, before its foreign keys change), so the wallet tables and their protection stay; to turn the wallet off, switch it off in the settings instead.

## Admin

- **Customers → Wallets** (web): every wallet with balance, credit limit and last activity, the totals, and each wallet's full ledger. With *manage* permission: manual credit/debit (a reason is required and the change is confirmed on a second screen), credit limit, freeze/unfreeze. Every change is in the audit log, written together with the change. A customer's wallet link (from the customer list) never creates a wallet just by being opened; a customer without one shows an empty wallet and, with *manage* permission, an "Open a wallet" button.
- **Mini App admin**: the wallet on a customer's page, its ledger, and an adjustment confirmed in Telegram's dialog.
- **Dashboard**: total customer balance and credit in use / granted.
- **Permissions**: *Wallets view* (super admin, admin, support) and *Wallets manage* (super admin, admin).
